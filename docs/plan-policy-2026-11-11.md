# Annual plan policy — 11 November 2026

| Region | Starter | Business |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Nigeria | NGN 200,000/year | NGN 350,000/year |
| Other countries | USD 250/year | USD 400/year |

Starter includes 500 products and up to five distinct active staff accounts. Business retains unlimited staff and products. Each account has one active POS session: signing in on another device ends its previous session and does not consume another allowance. Both plans cover one WooCommerce store and one physical location. Trial capacity remains full Business access.

Current pricing is used by marketing pages, plan comparison, checkout, account views, WordPress admin and operations admin. Backend checkout calculates the authoritative amount. Existing payment/receipt amounts and initialized checkout quotes remain unchanged; purchases and renewals initialized after rollout use the new catalogue. Existing Starter entitlements acquire the new staff allowance without resetting activation, domain binding or expiry.

Deploy the reviewed backend, website and operations admin together and distribute plugin 0.2.16 from the updated landing-page download. Installed stores need the plugin update for the five-account login ceiling. No database migration or rebilling is required. The hosted POS and native wrappers consume the shared WooCommerce entitlement policy; they contain no separate price or seat catalogue.

The earlier checkout-currency note records the prices in force on 10 November; this document supersedes its NGN Business price.
